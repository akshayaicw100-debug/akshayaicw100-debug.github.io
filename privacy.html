Privacy Policy

This page explains how Image Reducer handles information when you visit the website.

Last updated: October 2026. Review and update this policy if your website practices change.

Image processing

The image compression tool uses browser features to process the image selected by the visitor. The website code does not intentionally upload the selected image file to a server. Processing occurs on the visitor's device. Temporary preview URLs are created by the browser and released when the page is closed or the tool resets.

Information collected by hosting providers

This website is hosted using GitHub Pages. Hosting and infrastructure providers may process technical information such as IP address, browser details, request time, and requested pages as part of delivering and securing the website. Refer to the relevant provider's privacy information for details.

Cookies, analytics and advertising

This website may use third-party services in the future, such as analytics or advertising. Those services may use cookies, identifiers, or similar technologies subject to their own policies and applicable consent requirements. Do not add advertising or analytics scripts until you understand what information they collect and have updated this policy accordingly.

If Google AdSense is enabled, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to serve, measure, or personalize advertising as described in Google's policies and settings. Visitors can review Google's advertising and privacy controls. Update this section to match the actual services and consent configuration used on this site.

Children and sensitive information

This tool is a general-purpose utility and is not designed to collect personal information. Do not submit sensitive or confidential images on a device you do not trust.

External links

This website may link to third-party websites. We do not control their content or privacy practices. Review their policies before using them.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the website or its services change. The date above should be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

For privacy questions, use the contact details on our Contact page. Replace the placeholder email there with a real monitored address before publishing.
